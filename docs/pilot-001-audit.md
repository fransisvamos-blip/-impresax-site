## Current site — what works
- Rich factual content: services, studio philosophy, doctor profile, safety and sterilization.
- Contact information is repeated and easy to find once the user reaches the relevant sections.
- The service offering is broad and clearly documented.
- Real trust material exists: professional profile, ANDI association, accessibility, studio structure.
- Dedicated pages exist for specific topics such as home dental service, safety and professional guarantees.

## What feels old
- Very large navigation taxonomy with many nested service pages.
- Long text blocks before the user reaches the most commercially important information.
- Repetition of the same contact blocks across multiple pages.
- Content structure behaves like a directory of pages rather than a guided patient journey.
- The home page lists many services at the same visual priority, which weakens hierarchy.
- Copy is informative but frequently too dense for mobile scanning.

## What creates friction
- The path from landing to a clear next action is longer than necessary.
- The user must parse many treatment names before understanding the studio's core positioning.
- Too many navigation choices compete with appointment intent.
- Important trust information is spread across home, staff and guarantees pages instead of being synthesized before the CTA.
- No public online booking is currently available at the listed address; the conversion path therefore needs to optimize phone/email contact rather than simulate online booking.
- Public opening-hour sources are not perfectly consistent; publishing an unverified schedule would create avoidable trust risk.

## What we keep
- Business identity and Catanzaro location.
- Verified contacts.
- Broad verified service set.
- Respect/person-centered positioning.
- Professional profile of Dott. Francesco Giardinelli.
- ANDI association.
- Accessibility and studio-structure information.
- Safety/sterilization as a supporting trust theme.
- Home dental service as a distinctive verified service.

## What we remove
- Repetitive contact blocks.
- Excessive nested navigation in the first-level experience.
- Long encyclopedic copy on the home page.
- Equal visual weight for every treatment.
- Unverified or conflicting opening hours.

## What we rebuild
- Hero: who / where / patient value / contact in one screen.
- Trust hierarchy: verified credentials and studio facts before conversion.
- Service architecture: six primary service clusters plus expandable secondary services.
- Mobile conversion: persistent call/write actions.
- Doctor section: concise, verified professional profile.
- Contact section: no fake form and no patient-data collection.
- Premium editorial art direction without copying protected images from the existing site.

## Top 5 commercial improvements
1. Reduce time-to-understanding: location, professional identity and value proposition appear immediately.
2. Reduce time-to-contact: primary telephone action is visible in hero and mobile sticky CTA.
3. Move trust earlier: credentials, ANDI and accessibility become part of the decision path.
4. Simplify service discovery: grouped treatment areas replace a flat long list.
5. Remove conversion ambiguity: no fake booking form; phone/email are the real verified conversion channels.
