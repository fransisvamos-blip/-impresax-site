## Reusable rules
- No fake reviews, ratings, certifications, years, patients or results.
- No services outside the verified tenant dataset.
- No booking form unless the actual destination and authorization are verified.
- Prefer direct phone/email/WhatsApp only when each channel is verified.
- Do not publish opening hours when sources conflict.
- Patient trust appears before the strongest CTA.
- Primary service count on home: 4–8; secondary services remain discoverable without overwhelming the page.
- Mobile baseline: 390 px.
- Desktop baseline: 1440 px.
- External imagery must be generated, licensed or authorized.
- If authorized studio photography is unavailable, use a designed editorial system rather than copying source-site imagery.
